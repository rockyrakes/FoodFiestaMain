Our Journey

The Royal Timeline

📅 2022

The Idea is Born

Founders Rahul & Priya dream of creating a premium food delivery experience that brings restaurant-quality meals home.

A small kitchen in New Delhi starts serving 10 signature dishes. Word spreads like wildfire.
